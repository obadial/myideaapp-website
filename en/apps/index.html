Frequently asked questions

Are MyIdeApp apps free?

Yes, they're all free to download. Some offer an optional Premium, from €0.99/month, with its price shown on the app's page.

Do I need to create an account?

No. None of our apps require an account: you can use them as soon as you open them.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone. Your personal data (cycle, runs, spending, notes…) isn't sent to our servers and is never sold. Each app's privacy policy has the details.

Are the apps available on Android?

Not yet: they're on iPhone today, and the Android version is coming soon to Google Play.

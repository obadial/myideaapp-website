Frequently asked questions

Does RunFree work without internet?

Yes, fully offline: GPS, history and training plans with no connection and no cloud account.

Does RunFree upload my runs to the cloud?

No. Your routes, pace and records stay only on your device, with no social network and no comparisons.

Are there training plans?

Yes, 5K, 10K, half-marathon and marathon plans with guided weekly sessions.

Frequently asked questions

How do I log my water intake with HydroMe?

In one tap, with quick buttons +200ml, +350ml, +500ml. The progress ring fills up instantly.

Does HydroMe sync with Apple Health?

Yes, your intake syncs automatically with HealthKit every time you log.

Does HydroMe require an account?

No. No account, no cloud: all your data stays on your device.

MyIdeApp, an independent mobile app studio

MyIdeApp designs and publishes 12 apps, available today on iPhone: period tracking, running, hydration, budgeting, stock market practice, recipes, food journaling, sleep, breathing, affirmations, sexual health and a dating journal. One app, one job, done simply.

Our story

MyIdeApp was born in early May 2026 out of frustration. To track a cycle, drink enough water or follow spending, most apps asked you to create an account and hand over far too much data, for very little in return.

At MyIdeApp, we wanted a different approach: give people their information back, right on their phone, and let them use an app without creating an account. A bit like private browsing: you open it, you use it, and your information stays on your phone.

That became the studio's rule. Today it's 12 apps in 9 languages, from sexual health to the stock market, with the same promise: simple, useful, and your data stays with you.

What leaves your phone

To be transparent, these are the only outside services used, depending on the app:

  • Ads in the free versions (Google AdMob), based on your consent.
  • Premium subscriptions handled by Apple, with RevenueCat to verify the subscription.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (Firebase, Google) in some apps.
  • EnjoyMeal: if you set up your own key, a meal photo is sent to the AI service you chose to recognize it.
  • StockWatch: stock prices come from a market data provider.

The exact details are in each app's privacy policy.

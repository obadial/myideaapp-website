MyIdeApp, an independent iPhone app studio

MyIdeApp designs and publishes 12 iPhone apps: period tracking, running, hydration, budgeting, stock market practice, recipes, food journaling, sleep, breathing, affirmations, sexual health and a dating journal. One app, one job, done simply.

Who runs the studio

MyIdeApp is a studio based in France, founded and run by Lucas Obadia, an independent developer. He designs, builds and maintains every app himself, from the code to the App Store listings.

Our principles

Your data stays on your iPhone

Your cycle, runs, spending or notes are stored on your device, not on a server. Nothing is sold.

No account required

All 12 apps work without creating an account.

Clear prices

Free to download. The optional Premium price is shown clearly on every app page.

No judgment

Apps built for everyone, in 9 languages, with no stereotypes and no guilt.

What leaves your phone

To be transparent, these are the only outside services used, depending on the app:

  • Ads in the free versions (Google AdMob), based on your consent.
  • Premium subscriptions handled by Apple, with RevenueCat to verify the subscription.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (Firebase, Google) in some apps.
  • EnjoyMeal: if you set up your own key, a meal photo is sent to the AI service you chose to recognize it.
  • StockWatch: stock prices come from a market data provider.

The exact details are in each app's privacy policy.

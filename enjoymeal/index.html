Questions fréquentes

Comment EnjoyMeal calcule mes objectifs caloriques ?

À partir de formules scientifiques (Mifflin-St Jeor, recommandations OMS) adaptées à ton corps et à ton activité, sans logique de restriction.

Puis-je scanner mes aliments en photo ?

Oui, EnjoyMeal reconnaît tes aliments en photo grâce à l'IA et donne accès à plus de 300 000 aliments via Open Food Facts.

Mes données nutritionnelles sont-elles privées ?

Oui, elles restent sur ton appareil et les photos ne sont jamais envoyées vers nos serveurs.

Frequently asked questions

How does EnjoyMeal calculate my calorie goals?

With scientific formulas (Mifflin-St Jeor, WHO guidelines) adapted to your body and activity, without a restriction mindset.

Can I scan my food with a photo?

Yes, EnjoyMeal recognizes your food from a photo using AI and gives access to over 300,000 foods via Open Food Facts.

Is my nutrition data private?

Yes, it stays on your device and photos are never sent to our servers.

About EnjoyMeal (English)

EnjoyMeal is your inclusive, science-backed nutrition companion — designed to help you eat better, not less.

Food diary

Log every meal effortlessly. Search millions of foods from Open Food Facts, scan barcodes, enter custom foods, or let AI recognize your meal from a photo (Premium).

Calorie ring & macros

A beautiful visual ring shows your daily calorie progress. Track carbs, protein, fat, and fiber — all in one glance.

Personalized goals

Your daily calorie goal is calculated using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation — the gold standard recommended by dietitians. Set a gentle, realistic pace: no crash diets, no guilt.

Weight tracking

Log your weight over time and visualize your trend with an intuitive chart (Premium).

Insights & trends

See your average calories, macro balance, and logging streak. Unlock 30 and 90-day insights with Premium.

Nutritional goals

Customize your daily macro targets to match your personal goals — muscle building, weight loss, or maintenance.

AI PHOTO RECOGNITION (Premium)

Take a photo of your meal and let AI identify the foods automatically. Supports Claude (Anthropic) and Gemini (Google) — your API key, stored securely on your device.

EXPORT PDF (Premium)

Download a beautiful PDF summary of your nutrition data to share with your dietitian.

Meal reminders

Get gentle reminders to log breakfast, lunch, and dinner at the times that work for you.

Sport coach

Get moving with a curated catalog of cardio, strength, and mobility workouts — or create your own. Set flexible reminders by day and time, log a session in one tap, and watch your calorie budget adjust to reflect what you've burned.

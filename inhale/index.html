About Inhale (English)

Breathe better. Feel better.

Inhale brings you science-backed breathing techniques trusted by doctors, athletes and psychologists — in just 5 minutes a day.

Your breath is the only body function you can consciously control in real time. When you change your breathing, you directly influence your heart rate, nervous system and mental state. Inhale harnesses this power with techniques proven by science.

Free techniques

  • Cardiac Coherence (5-5) — The gold standard for stress reduction, endorsed by the HeartMath Institute and Dr David Servan-Schreiber. 5 minutes is enough to reset your nervous system.
  • Box Breathing (4-4-4-4) — Used by Navy SEALs, SWAT teams and top-level athletes to stay calm and focused under extreme pressure.
  • 4-7-8 Breathing — Dr Andrew Weil's famous technique for rapid anxiety relief and falling asleep faster at night.
  • Physiological Sigh — Stanford's discovery: the fastest way to reduce stress. A double inhale followed by a long exhale works in seconds.

Premium features

Unlock 20+ advanced techniques including Wim Hof, Nadi Shodhana, Kapalabhati and Combat Breathing.

  • 5 themed 30-day programs — Anxiety, Sleep, Focus, Sport and Pain Relief. Follow a proven daily plan and build lasting breathing habits.
  • SOS mode — Instant calm in 60 seconds. Accessible from anywhere, even the lock screen, for panic and acute stress moments.
  • Advanced statistics — Track your sessions, streaks and progress with beautiful charts.
  • Smart reminders — Personalised daily breathing reminders at the time that works best for you.

Why inhale?

Simple on the surface. Profound in science. You feel better from the very first session — no yoga or meditation background needed.

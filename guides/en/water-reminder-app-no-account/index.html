A water reminder app with no account: HydroMe, WaterMinder or Plant Nanny?

By MyIdeApp, the studio behind HydroMe. Comparison based on each app's official pages. Read in FR · ES

The short answer

HydroMe is the simplest: one tap to log a drink, a ring that fills up, a streak to keep, for free and with no account. WaterMinder suits you if you want very detailed tracking with Apple Watch. Plant Nanny turns hydration into a game: you grow a virtual plant as you drink.

Why choose HydroMe

  • No account, no data sent: everything stays on your iPhone.
  • Free: daily goal, streak, 6 drink types, weekly history, 3 reminders.
  • Coffee and tea don't count as water: each drink has its own coefficient.
  • ml or oz units, 9 languages.

When another app is a better fit

If you want to track hydration from your watch, WaterMinder has an Apple Watch app, which HydroMe doesn't. If you need a game to stick to the habit, Plant Nanny is more motivating.

Frequently asked questions

Does HydroMe require an account?

No, and no data leaves your phone.

How much water should I drink a day?

It depends on your weight, activity and climate. HydroMe lets you set your own daily goal; ask a health professional if in doubt.

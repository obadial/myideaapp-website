Food tracking without dieting or guilt: EnjoyMeal, MyFitnessPal or Yazio?

By MyIdeApp, the studio behind EnjoyMeal. Comparison based on each app's official pages. Read in FR · ES

The short answer

EnjoyMeal tracks your meals, calories and macros with gentle goals and no crash diets, and barcode scanning is free. MyFitnessPal and Yazio are very complete calorie counters with huge food databases, but their barcode scanner is for subscribers only.

Why choose EnjoyMeal

  • Free: meal diary, barcode scanning, calories and macros, personalised goals, reminders.
  • Goals computed with the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, at a gentle pace, no crash diets.
  • A built-in sport coach: cardio, strength and mobility sessions.
  • Premium ($3.99/month or $24.99/year) adds AI photo logging, weight tracking and PDF export.

When another app is a better fit

If you follow a precise training program and want to connect many fitness apps, MyFitnessPal has the largest ecosystem. If you practice intermittent fasting, Yazio has a built-in timer. EnjoyMeal is an informational tool and doesn't replace advice from a health professional.

Frequently asked questions

Does EnjoyMeal count calories?

Yes, with a calorie ring and macro tracking, but with realistic rather than restrictive goals.

Where does the food data come from?

From the open Open Food Facts database, under the ODbL license.

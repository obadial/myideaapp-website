Which app to keep your recipes offline? MyFoodBook, Paprika or Apple Notes

By MyIdeApp, the studio behind MyFoodBook. Comparison based on each app's official pages. Read in FR · ES

The short answer

MyFoodBook is a free recipe keeper that works fully offline with no account, built for your own recipes with photos, steps and tags. Paprika, a one-time purchase, goes further if you want to import recipes from websites and plan meals. Apple Notes is enough if you just jot down a few recipes as free text.

Why choose MyFoodBook

  • Truly free: no recipe feature is paywalled.
  • Everything stays on your iPhone, even with no signal in the kitchen.
  • Photos of your dishes taken in the app, free-form tags (vegan, dessert, quick…).
  • The optional Premium only removes ads.

When another app is a better fit

MyFoodBook doesn't import recipes from websites and has no meal planner. If that is what you need, Paprika is the better choice, as a one-time purchase with no subscription. If you only have a handful of recipes, Apple Notes does the job with nothing to install.

Frequently asked questions

Is MyFoodBook really free?

Yes. Every feature is free; the app is supported by ads.

Are my recipes backed up online?

No, they stay on your device and follow your iPhone backup.

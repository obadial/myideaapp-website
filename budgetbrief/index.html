Questions fréquentes

MyBudgetBrief est-elle connectée à ma banque ?

Non, jamais. Tes données financières ne quittent jamais ton iPhone : aucun cloud, aucun serveur, aucun partage tiers.

Puis-je fixer un budget par catégorie ?

Oui, tu définis un plafond mensuel par catégorie (alimentation, transport, loisirs…) et tu suis ta progression en temps réel.

MyBudgetBrief gère-t-elle les dépenses récurrentes ?

Oui, planifie une fois tes loyers, abonnements et factures, puis oublie-les.

About MyBudgetBrief (English)

MyBudgetBrief — Know Where Your Money Goes

Track every expense in seconds. Set monthly budgets by category. See exactly where your money is going — without connecting your bank account.

MyBudgetBrief is privacy-first: all your data stays on your device. No ads tracking your purchases, no data sold to third parties.

Smart budget dashboard

See your spending vs. budget for each category at a glance. Color-coded progress bars show you when you're on track (green), approaching your limit (orange), or over budget (red).

Fast expense logging

Add an expense in under 5 seconds. Amount, category, optional note — done. Your history is always at your fingertips.

Monthly statistics

Beautiful charts show your spending patterns over time. Understand your habits, adjust your budget, and build better financial routines.

8 Expense Categories

Food · Transport · Housing · Health · Entertainment · Shopping · Travel · Other

100% private

No bank connection, no cloud sync. All your financial data stays on your device, encrypted by your phone's security. Use anonymously — no account required.

MyBudgetBrief Pro unlocks:

  • Unlimited history (free: last 3 months)
  • PDF export for any month
  • Recurring expenses tracking
  • 12-month statistics & trends
  • Ad-free experience

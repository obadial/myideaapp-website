About HydroMe (English)

HydroMe is a minimalist hydration tracker built around one simple idea: staying hydrated should be effortless.

Tap once to log a drink. Watch your progress ring fill up. Keep your streak alive.

No cloud sync required. No account needed. All your data stays on your device — always.

What's included (free)

  • Log water and drinks in one tap
  • Beautiful animated progress ring
  • Daily goal with streak counter
  • 6 drink types with hydration coefficients (water, coffee, tea, juice, sports drinks)
  • Weekly history view
  • Up to 3 daily reminders
  • Metric (ml) and imperial (oz) units
  • 9 languages supported

Hydrome pro

Unlock the full experience:

◆ 30-day hydration statistics with detailed charts

◆ iOS Home Screen Widget — see your progress at a glance

◆ Apple Health sync — water intake recorded automatically

◆ Smart reminders — up to 5 reminders, intelligently spaced throughout your day

◆ Ad-free experience

Science-backed

HydroMe uses hydration coefficients per drink type. Coffee counts less (diuretic effect), sports drinks count fully. It's not just about water — it's about real hydration.

Privacy first

Your health data never leaves your device. No servers. No cloud. No tracking. HydroMe works completely offline.

Simple. beautiful. yours.

HydroMe was built for people who want to build better habits without the complexity. One ring. One goal. One streak.

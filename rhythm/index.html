About Rhythm (English)

Rhythm helps you understand your menstrual cycle on your own terms — without judgment, without shame.

Know your cycle

Rhythm predicts your next period, ovulation window, and all four cycle phases (menstrual, follicular, ovulation, luteal) based on your personal data. See your full month at a glance on the calendar.

Log what matters

Track what you experience each day: flow intensity, pain level (0–10), symptoms (cramps, bloating, headaches, breast tenderness, and more), mood, energy, and personal notes. Rhythm remembers everything so you don't have to.

Medication reminders

Add your pill, patch, ring, or any supplement. Mark each one taken directly from your daily log. Track your adherence over time — Rhythm shows how consistent you've been, always without judgment.

Apple health

Connect Rhythm to Apple Health to sync your menstrual flow data automatically. Your health, all in one place.

Home screen widget

See your current phase, cycle day, and days until your next period right from your home screen. Available in small, medium, and Lock Screen sizes for iOS 16+.

Calendar sync

Export your predicted period, ovulation day, and fertile window to any native calendar — iCloud, Google Calendar, or any CalDAV-compatible service.

Insights

Track your average cycle and period length over time. Spot patterns in your body at a glance.

Privacy first

Your cycle data is deeply personal. Rhythm stores everything locally on your device. We never sell your data, never track you, and never share your information with third parties. You can even use Rhythm without creating an account.

Premium

Go further with Rhythm Premium:

— Advanced Insights: symptom trends, mood history, and energy & wellbeing analysis by cycle phase

— Export your full cycle history as a PDF to share with your doctor

— Ad-free experience

Rhythm is for everyone who has a cycle. No assumptions. No labels. No stereotypes. Just your body, your data, your rhythm.
